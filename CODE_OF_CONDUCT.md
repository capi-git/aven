# Aven community guidelines

Be respectful, keep feedback constructive, and make room for different levels of experience. Discuss the work without personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted contact, and sharing someone else's private information are not welcome. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.

The maintainer may remove content, limit participation, or block contributors whose conduct harms others. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and protect the people affected.

For a conduct concern, use GitHub's **Report content** action on the relevant content. Do not post another person's private information in a public report.
